# The whole: behaviour from the interaction of many systems

**Started:** 2026-10-31, at the owner's direction. This document is the map for the line of work; each part has its
own preregistration under `docs/prereg/` and its own decisions entry. It is amended by dated notes, never rewritten.

## The hypothesis

The owner's working hypothesis: mammalian behaviour emerges from the interaction of many systems, so studying each
part on its own may fail where the whole works. The project's history argues for testing it rather than assuming it:
the legacy profile was the everything-interacting version, and its best behaviour came from interactions that were
faults (`docs/research_plan.md` §1); later, three artefacts made the agent look smarter than it was (G46–G52). So each
part of this work names its target behaviour before any run, and brackets the whole by removing each part from it and
adding each part alone to the base: emergence from interaction shows as a behaviour the whole has, no single
addition has, and most removals break.

## The parts

Every part the audit graded "cartoon" is upgraded to a referenced role and coupled to the others, all opt-in
(`docs/prereg/whole_resource.md` §2): metabolism and interoception (reward as drive reduction), motivation, the four
neuromodulators in their roles, homeostatic sleep, criticality as a state variable, replay of the successor map, the
action value beside the SR, the rodent body and the orienting response. The research profile's instrument is the
base.
